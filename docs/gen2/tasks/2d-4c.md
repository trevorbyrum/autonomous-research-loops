## Required
1. **Durable check records.**
   - Store each V-8 check (numeric units, denominators, negation, surrounding qualifications) as an immutable, **attributed** record. Each record carries:
     - the check kind;
     - the outcome (`succeeded`, `failed`, `not_applicable`, `not_checked` or `unavailable`);
     - the performing invocation, which is the verifier and never the producer;
     - the exact cited span or spans and the source artifact it examined;
     - the method or version used, with any retained evidence.
   - **Record and bind relationships only.** Don't claim semantic correctness the store can't establish.
2. **Receipts consume the records.**
   - A load-bearing `supports` or `partially_supports` receipt must reference a durable check record for every kind it marks `succeeded` or `not_applicable`.
   - Each record must bind to the receipt's claim revision, cited spans, source artifact and verifier.
   - The receipt JSON's check status must equal the record's outcome. Self-declared statuses no longer carry authority on their own.
   - Truthful unsuccessful verdicts may still record checks as not performed or unavailable.
3. **Promotion (V-4).** The consuming gate must read the durable records, so that no adjudication waives an unperformed check. Keep every existing gate: tier, use, the SQL 34 bound basis, and tier-0.
4. **If the design needs a choice the authority doesn't settle,** stop after DOSSIER. Report INCOMPLETE with the open question and your recommendation. Examples: what evidence counts as performing a check; whether `not_applicable` needs a reason.
5. **Tests.**
   - Router and raw-DDL tests for each kind:
     - a missing record;
     - a foreign claim, span or verifier;
     - the producer performing the check;
     - a status that doesn't match the record;
     - each outcome.
   - Accepted controls.
   - Promotion with and without records.
   - Replay/conflict and rollback.
   - Predicate mutants with control path traces.
   - A receipt-fixture audit: every positive load-bearing receipt needs records.
