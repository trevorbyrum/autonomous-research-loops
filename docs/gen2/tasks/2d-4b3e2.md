## Required
1. **The tool registry** (operator ruling):
   - a versioned, immutable, operator-maintained registry, with a canonical identity and family for each tool;
   - additions and changes go through audited operator authority, never through an outcome;
   - `source_extractions` must reference a registered tool, and unknown, aliased or case-variant names are refused at the Router and in raw DDL;
   - define the successor validation policy (`extraction-validation/2`): independence means a **different registered family**, and same-family replay is integrity only.
   - Existing `/1` records must stay readable and must never become authority.
2. **Receipts consume the records,** on both extraction paths:
   - A `validated_extraction` receipt must reference a durable `extraction_validations` row that succeeded under the `/2` policy and is bound to the same acquisition, work, version and verifier lineage.
   - A `verifier_extraction` receipt must reference the verifier's own durable `source_extractions` row, bound the same way.
   - Invented, unbound, failed, same-family or `/1`-only references are refused at the Router and in raw DDL.
   - The free-text `validation_ref` no longer carries authority.
   - **Admission alone grants no promotion. SQL 17 stays in force.**
3. **Docs.** BOUNDARIES, INVARIANTS (V-3) and DOSSIER must state exactly what is enforced and that SQL 17 still fences.
4. **Tests.**
   - Registry admission and refusal, including aliases, case variants and unknown tools.
   - Receipts referencing each kind of invalid basis, plus accepted controls for both methods.
   - Raw-DDL equivalents.
   - Readback, replay/conflict and rollback.
   - A predicate-level paired mutant for each guard.
   - The receipt-fixture audit: every positive extraction receipt control needs a durable basis now.
