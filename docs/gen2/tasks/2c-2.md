2. **Source, version and study lineage:**
   - Identity deduplication never merges distinct papers that share a study origin (E-3).

3. **Dated source lifecycle dispositions** (add, keep, supersede, coexist, quarantine, duplicate-report; flow S4.5), plus **contradiction and branch dispositions.** Each one is dated, attributed and reversible by a later record. Nothing is overwritten.
4. **Semantic accrual operands:**
   - Accounting reads these new inventories alongside the 2c-1 inventories, inside the same Router `_snapshot()`.
   - Branch accrual and labeled novelty come out as typed operands for 2c-3.

5. **Tests:**
   - Expected values written by hand.
   - Router-level admission tests, including refusals: an unauthorized actor, a missing revision or framing, and an attempt to overwrite.
   - **Fixtures:**
     - two papers that share a study origin: 2 works, with one shared origin;
     - a lifecycle chain of supersede, then a later reversal;
     - the labels consumed by novelty.
   - Hypothesis properties where a general law holds. For example, appending a later disposition never changes an earlier dated view.
