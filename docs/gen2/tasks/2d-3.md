**What's missing:** the engine currently trusts match labels it is given. It must compute the byte, span and normalization check itself, persist the result, notify the capture owner, and stop a mismatch from being adjudicated past.

## V-6 (the invariant)
- Quoted bytes are checked against a **versioned source artifact**, with **explicit normalization** and **span offsets**.
- A mismatch quarantines the **quote**, not the source. It also notifies the owner of the capturing pass, and blocks the quote from backing any disposition until it is re-captured.
- **An adjudication can resolve an NLI alarm, never a byte mismatch.**

## Operator ruling: layered matching, made consistent with V-6
1. **Exact bytes** against the artifact span: `match`.
2. **Named, versioned normalization, then exact**: `match_normalized`.
   - The normalization handles whitespace, line-break hyphenation, curly vs straight quotes, dash variants, and Unicode compatibility forms (cite UAX #15 NFKC, or whichever standard form you use).
   - Keep the original bytes, the normalized form, and the normalization's name and version.
3. **Still different but ≥98% similar** after normalization: a **near match**.
   - Treat it as a **mismatch** under V-6: quarantine it, notify the owner, and **never auto-pass it**.
   - Record a `near_match` diagnostic with its similarity score and the differing spans, so a reviewer can **re-capture** the exact text quickly.
   - Per V-6 it can't be adjudicated into a pass. "Held for review" means the review leads to a re-capture.
4. **Any difference in a number, a negation word or a unit always fails as a plain mismatch**, whatever the similarity. There is no near-match diagnostic for these. Use explicit, versioned token lists (negation words, unit symbols) and number parsing, and cite your sources for them.
5. **The similarity measure must be a named, established one.** Prefer an established, pinned library (for example rapidfuzz's normalized Levenshtein) over a custom implementation. If you add a dependency, pin it through the repo's existing dependency mechanism and say so. If you use stdlib `difflib`, justify why its ratio is adequate.
