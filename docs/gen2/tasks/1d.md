## Deliverables
1. **Question registry (D-1, D-4).** Loading, pinning and restart retention of the frozen question-ID/content/version/hash format, with fake or disabled providers only (D-7: the engine runs with the decision layer disabled). A DecisionSpec's pinned question/version/hash must resolve against the registry; an unknown or altered question refuses.
2. **Qualification / revocation fences (D-4, D-5).** Fake qualification and revocation records exercise the authority fences end to end: no qualification ⇒ shadow/advisory at most; revocation takes effect; a non-null ref that isn't in the registry is not qualification. No live providers.
3. **Config-bundle registry (RG-9, G-10).** Versioned, validated, activated bundles; in-flight work keeps its pinned bundle; restart preserves pins. The policy values now hardcoded as defaults move here: the supervisor `Policy` values (including `lock_wait_s`, refusal budget 3, unknown-episode 5/600 s), and the router's one-hour `outcome_unknown` hold window. Defaults stay as shipped; the bundle overrides them.
4. **Reservations and signal-queue budget/cooldown (G-5, §13).** Protected-exploration and auto-promotion budget reservations, and the coalesced signal queue's budget and cooldown, as store/router state with the checks G-5 names (revision-bound, within the reservation). Fake-executor exercise only.
5. **Brief/amendment versioning (G-4; the 1b bootstrap ruling).** Minimal router-owned commands: re-version a brief, reassign its owner, extend its deadline (a new version each time — versions stay immutable), and record a contract amendment proposal/approval as the existing schema and DDL already define. 1e exposes these to the operator; here they're exercised in-process.
6. **G-1 amendment impact and stale-work handling (from the 1a review; replaces 1b's interim fail-closed).** When a contract revision or brief version supersedes the one in-flight work is pinned to: identify the affected work, dossiers and verifications; apply the compatibility/staleness rules (G-1, G-6's stale-under-new-framing vocabulary where it applies); admitted work either completes under its pins within its deadline or is fenced, per C-10/C-12 semantics; nothing silently reuses stale pins, and nothing is silently discarded. `amendment_pending` becomes the documented narrow case, not the whole answer.
7. **Scheduling/retry of failed and cancelled work (1c deferral).** The minimal 1d version: an operator- or policy-driven re-queue path through the router that respects budgets and incidents; not an autonomous scheduler.
