## Operator rulings, restated
1. **Vocabulary A:**
   - The obligation dispositions are **supported, contradicted, unresolved and deferred**, plus a separate **open** (not-yet-assessed) state.
   - Every contradiction record and every deferral record is preserved in full.
   - There is no automatic open→unresolved transition.
2. **Confidence A:**
   - Each contract revision declares its own **named categorical confidence scheme**: an id, a version, the allowed labels, and a definition (rubric) for each label.
   - The scheme is bound into the hash-locked contract and approved with it.
   - **The engine ships no built-in scale.** Schema fixtures may include an example only.
   - Labels are attributed judgments, never probabilities.
   - Unknown confidence is `{basis: unknown, reason}`.
