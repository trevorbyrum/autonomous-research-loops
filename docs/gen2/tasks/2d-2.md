**DEBT-032 (2c-4b N1):**
- The 2c-4b coverage and depth views accept `BranchLink` and `WindowPin` as explicit snapshot operands. That means a pure caller could invent them.
- Before dossier assembly uses branch coverage, the Router-owned path must do three things:
  - obtain a **durable, attributable branch/window association**. A new table isn't required; any admitted, auditable record will do;
  - **validate** the topic, the branch identity and the referenced research window;
  - **derive window revisions from the durable invocation and contract records**, not from consumer-chosen values.
- Missing or unverified provenance stays **unknown**.
- Task 2e2 will emit these records through this admission path.

2. **Validation:**
   - The branch must exist in the 2c-2b2a registry for the same topic.
   - The window must be a real research invocation of that topic.
   - Cross-topic, forged or mismatched associations and pins are refused.
3. **A read adapter** that turns admitted associations into the views' `BranchLink` and `WindowPin` inside the existing snapshot.
   - Window revisions are derived from the invocation and contract records.
   - Absent associations stay unknown.
   - Pure caller-supplied operands remain possible for fixtures, but they confer no authority. Say so in the docs.
4. **Tests, with expected values written by hand:**
   - a real recorded association that yields the expected view;
   - refusal of forged, cross-topic and mismatched associations and pins;
   - absent evidence that stays unknown;
   - a paired mutant for each guard.
