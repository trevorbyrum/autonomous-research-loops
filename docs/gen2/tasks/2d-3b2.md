# 2d-3b part 2: governed quote verification (V-6)

**Plan:** `docs/gen2/tasks/2d-3b.md` items 2–3; `docs/gen2/DOSSIER.md` §2d-3b. Part 1 (DEBT-038, `quote-protection/5`) was accepted at `dba8f35`, and the matcher is ready to wire in.

## Required
1. **The engine computes the quote check itself, inside the existing governed path.**
   - Never trust a caller-supplied match label.
   - Derive the artifact identity (hash and source version) from the recorded work and source version.
   - Call the accepted pure matcher.
2. **Persistence.** Store the computed result in `quote_checks` (SQL 03), together with:
   - the original bytes, the normalized forms and the span;
   - the version identities (normalization, protection, decimal, negation, unit);
   - any `near_match` diagnostic, with its score and the spans that differ.

   Writes are append-only and replay-safe.
3. **V-6 consequences.**
   - A mismatch, including a near match, **quarantines the quote**, not the source.
   - It **notifies the owner of the capturing pass** through the existing records.
   - It **blocks the quote from backing any disposition** until it is re-captured.
   - **An adjudication can resolve only an NLI alarm, never a byte mismatch.** Refuse any other attempt.
   - A re-capture that matches clears the block for the new capture.
4. **Placement.** Put this in a collaborator, either the existing one or a new one registered in the composition test. `service.py` is at 1,420 of 1,500 lines, so give it thin wiring only (DEBT-036).
5. **Tests, end to end through the Router, with expected values written by hand.** Cover:
   - an exact match;
   - a normalized match;
   - a near match, quarantined with its diagnostic;
   - a protected mismatch;
   - the wrong artifact version or a forged hash;
   - a supplied label being ignored;
   - adjudication of a mismatch being refused;
   - an NLI-alarm adjudication being allowed;
   - the owner being notified;
   - a re-capture that clears the block;
   - replay.

   Give each guard a paired mutant.
